# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment for everyone
participating in devengine.

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project. Harassment,
personal attacks, discrimination, threats, publication of private information,
and other abusive behavior are not tolerated.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict
participation when behavior is inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately
to `andreunix@proton.me`; reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical. Retaliation against good-faith reports is prohibited.
